Frequently asked questions

Can I play without the internet?

Yes. The core Baagchal game supports offline play.

How can two people play?

Choose Pass & Play to alternate turns on the same iPhone.

How do I play against the computer?

Choose Play vs Computer from the main screen.

How do I start a new game?

Use Restart Game in the game screen.
