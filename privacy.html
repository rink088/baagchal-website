Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Baagchal iPhone game and its support contact handle information.

1. Core game

Baagchal's Play vs Computer and Pass & Play modes work offline and do not require you to create an account. You do not need to submit personal information to play these modes.

2. Information you choose to send

If you contact us at paperworkjapan.support@gmail.com, we receive the information you include, such as your email address, message, and any attachments. We use this information to respond to support requests and troubleshoot reported problems.

3. Game data and device services

Core gameplay runs on your device. Version 1.0 provides local, offline game modes. Device operating-system services and Apple's App Store or TestFlight may process diagnostics or other information according to your device settings and Apple's own policies. Such services are operated by Apple, not by this support website.

4. Website information

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may process technical information associated with website access under its own policies. We do not include advertising, website analytics scripts, or contact forms in these pages.

5. Sharing and retention

We do not sell information you provide through support. Support messages may be retained as needed to handle your request and maintain records of support correspondence. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us, subject to applicable obligations.

6. Children

Baagchal does not require an account or personal information for its core game modes. If a parent or guardian contacts support about a child, please avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date when appropriate.
